The current mega-menu lists Solar/Energy Solutions (Hybrid Solar, Off-Grid Solar, Solar Tubewell, Solar Street Light), Energy Solutions (BESS, EV Charger), Security & Access Control (CCTV, electric security fence, intrusion detection, biometric access, gate automation, boom gates, EAS), Smart Home Automation (environment control, energy conservation, smart panels/switches, gate automation, automated doorbell), and Fire Safety & Fire Fighting (addressable/conventional alarms, sprinklers, hose reels, fire/smoke/gas detectors, CO2 and DCP extinguishers).

## Quality and content risks

- Duplicate keyword-stuffed H1/H2 headings and repeated navigation markup.
- Spelling/grammar errors (“Radiance” misspellings, “provdie”, inconsistent capitalization).
- Industrial capacity conflicts: 1 MW on the Solutions page versus 2 MW+ on homepage/current article.
- Partner logos are images without identifiable names in accessible text.
- Projects lack accessible case-study details.
- Some blog claims, prices and incentives are dated or unsupported.
- No visible privacy-policy content; this concept provides a clearly labelled placeholder.
- Several pages timed out intermittently during audit.
